When I was a kid I wanted to be a writer. At first I imagined myself as an author and later as a journalist. Neither of those happened if you can't tell by my current writing style (or lack thereof). Instead, I started programming in high school, went to college for Computer Science, and have been a Software Engineer of some variety since graduating. I don't regret this but I do think it leaves something to be desired creatively. There are times that I feel very creative in my work, but it's usually expressed as code that only my coworkers ever see and works behind the scenes. Website design can be fun, but I'm also not a designer and I don't pretend to have a great eye for aesthetics.

For a while, I wasn't really reading and definitely wasn't writing. My wife and I have made a big effort the past few years to make reading a habit (it's more of an effort for me than her). The more I read, the more I have an itch to write down my own thoughts. This blog is my attempt to practice writing and expressing myself in a way I've always wanted to but never sat down to do. My only goals are to refine the ideas in my head and to get better at this. We'll see how it goes.
